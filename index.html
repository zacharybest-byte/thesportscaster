Every voice deserves to be heard

Malcom Harris-Gowdie has autism and cerebral palsy, and a recall of sports statistics he calls his superpower. For four years he has reported for FanSided alongside Special Olympics.

The Sportscaster follows Malcom through Super Bowl Media Week as he works the scrum for his questions and interviews some of football’s biggest names — George Kittle, Christian McCaffrey, Baker Mayfield. This is his fourth Super Bowl, and he still hasn’t been offered a full-time job as a broadcaster. A film about a reporter already doing the work, waiting for someone to open the door.
