Every voice deserves to be heard

For four years, FanSided has worked alongside Special Olympics athlete Malcom Harris-Gowdie — a reporter with autism and cerebral palsy whose recall of sports statistics is his superpower.

The Sportscaster follows Malcom at Super Bowl Media Week as he interviews some of football’s biggest names — celebrating his talent, determination and passion, and reinforcing that everyone deserves the opportunity to participate, pursue their passions, and be heard.
